Medical disclaimer

This platform connects people; it does not replace hospitals or blood banks. Always confirm donor eligibility with a medical professional.
  • We do not give medical advice, and nothing here is a diagnosis.
  • Eligibility information on this site is general and may differ from the rules of your local blood bank or national blood service. Always follow their guidance.
  • Every donor is checked privately by trained staff at the blood bank or hospital before any donation.
  • An organ pledge records a wish. It is not a legal contract. Local law and your family's decision may apply, and hospitals and transplant authorities handle any matching.
  • Donation is always free. Never pay a donor, and report anyone who asks for money.

See also our privacy policy.
