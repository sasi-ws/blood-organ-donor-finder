Privacy policy

We keep this short and simple. We collect only what is needed to connect donors with people who need blood.

What we store

  • Your account: username, email, and a protected (hashed) password.
  • Donor details: name, date of birth, gender, blood group, donor type, city, district, phone number, and last donation date.
  • The only health-related data we keep is your blood group, donor type, availability, and last donation date. We never ask about illnesses, medicines, or private habits. The blood bank checks those privately.
  • Your messages, contact requests, and reports you send through the platform.

Who can see your information

  • Search results show only your name, blood group, city, and Verified status.
  • Your phone number is shown only to a person you have accepted. It is never shown in search results, on public request pages, or in share messages.
  • Administrators can see your profile so they can verify accounts and review reports.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time.
  • You can pause your listing so you are hidden from search without losing anything.
  • You can delete your account. This permanently removes your profile, your contact requests, and your pledges.

Consent

We list you as a donor only after you tick the consent box. You can withdraw your consent by pausing or deleting your profile.

Money

Blood donation is always free. We never charge for donor contact, and nobody may ask you for payment. Please report anyone who does.

This is a student project that currently uses fictional data. Read the medical disclaimer too.
