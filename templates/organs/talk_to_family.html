Talk to My Family

Start a meaningful conversation about your organ donation wishes with the people who matter to you.

Why talk to your family?

Sharing your organ donation wishes helps your family understand your decision and know what matters to you. A calm, honest conversation can help everyone feel informed and prepared.

Remember: This guide supports family discussion. A pledge records your intention and does not guarantee donation or determine medical eligibility.

How to start the conversation

1. Choose a comfortable time

Find a quiet moment when everyone has time to listen and talk without feeling rushed.

2. Explain your wishes clearly

Explain why organ donation matters to you and what you hope your family will understand.

3. Listen to their questions

Give your family time to share their feelings and ask questions. It is okay if everyone needs more time to think.

4. Find reliable information together

Discuss any concerns and consult an appropriate healthcare professional or authorised donation service for accurate information.

Useful talking points

You could start with one of these sentences:

“I want to share my thoughts about organ donation because this is important to me.”
“I would like you to understand my wishes and the reasons behind my decision.”
“What questions or concerns do you have about organ donation?”
“Can we find reliable information together so we can understand the process better?”
