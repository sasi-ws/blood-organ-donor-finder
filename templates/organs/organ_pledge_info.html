Organ Pledge Information

An organ pledge records your intention to donate organs or tissues in the future. It is important to understand your decision and discuss it with your family.

Before You Make a Pledge

  • Learn about organ and tissue donation.
  • Discuss your wishes with your family.
  • Provide accurate family contact information.
  • Read the consent statement before submitting.

What Happens Next?

You can select the organs or tissues you wish to pledge and provide a family contact. This form records your intention; it does not confirm medical eligibility or guarantee that donation will take place.
