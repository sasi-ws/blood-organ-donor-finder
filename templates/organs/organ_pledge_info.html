What a pledge means

An organ pledge records your wish to donate organs or tissues after your death. It is not a legal contract, and it does not match you with a patient.

Your family is usually asked to confirm your wish, so please talk to them first. We will send your family contact a short information email.

Before you make a pledge

  • Learn about organ and tissue donation.
  • Discuss your wishes with your family.
  • Give accurate family contact details.
  • Read the consent statement before submitting.

What happens next

You choose the organs or tissues you wish to pledge and add a family contact. This form records your intention. It does not confirm medical eligibility or guarantee that donation will take place. You can edit or withdraw it at any time.
